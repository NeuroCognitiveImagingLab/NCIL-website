Do children's brains respond to punctuation while they read? We recorded EEG from
children in grades 3–5 as they silently read short stories containing correct,
incorrect and missing commas, and looked for the closure positive shift — a brain
response that marks phrase boundaries in adults.

Presented at the Society for the Neurobiology of Language (SNL) Annual Meeting, 2026.
