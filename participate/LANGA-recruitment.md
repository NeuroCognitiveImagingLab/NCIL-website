# Second Language Acquisition
{:.center}

We are recruiting Dalhousie students through SONA. Please continue reading for more information!

## What Is the Purpose of the Project?
The LANGA (LANguage GAming) project studies what changes in the brain as someone first learns a new language. We have built language-learning games modelled on popular apps, and we record brain activity using EEG while participants play them — which lets us watch vocabulary and grammar being learned as it happens, rather than only testing what was learned afterwards. Read more about the [LANGA project here](https://ncil.science/projects/LANGA).

## What Does a Participant Do?
Participants take part at the NCIL lab, located in the Life Sciences Centre on Dalhousie University's Studley campus. You will play a language-learning game on a computer while we record your brain activity using EEG. EEG involves wearing a soft cap fitted with electrodes; a water-soluble gel is used to bridge the gap between the scalp and each electrode. It is safe and painless.
