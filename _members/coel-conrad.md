Coel Conrad, PhD is an Associate Professor at Dalhousie’s Faculty of Management and College of Digital Transformation. They have interdisciplinary expertise that spans the management, computer science, and cognitive neuroscience disciplines. They oversee projects about information technology use and cognition, usually related to educational technology, social media, or artificial intelligence. They are particularly interested in supervising or co-supervising students who want to pursue projects related to the cognitive and affective processes which affect how people use mobile or web technologies.
